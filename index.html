Calculation and interpretation

MAP is estimated as DBP + (SBP − DBP)/3, assuming a typical cardiac cycle at rest. Pulse pressure = SBP − DBP; RPP = HR × SBP; CPP = MAP − ICP. Values are estimates, not direct measurements of tissue perfusion or clinical diagnoses.

Context matters: hemodynamics, rhythm, measurement method and individual clinical targets affect interpretation. Do not use a MAP value alone to diagnose shock or hypertensive emergency, or to make treatment decisions.
